# Lecturer User Guide

1. Sign in with the lecturer account created by an administrator.
2. View the courses assigned to you.
3. Create assignments for the relevant course, supplying a title/instructions, deadline and maximum mark.
4. View submissions for your courses and inspect the submitted file reference.
5. Mark each submission with a mark, feedback and the required audit reason.
6. To extend a deadline, choose the individual-extension option and identify the student, or choose the whole-class extension option to apply one deadline to all students enrolled in the assignment's course. Supply the new deadline and a reason. You must be an active lecturer assigned to the course.
7. The whole-class extension is the default deadline for enrolled students who do not have an individual extension. A student's individual extension takes precedence over the class-wide deadline.
8. Submit marks through the moderation workflow. A head of department must moderate/approve and release marks before students can see them.
9. Choose the password-change option when needed; choose logout to end the session.

Check the assignment maximum mark and deadline carefully before marking. The system applies its configured late-penalty rules; do not manually apply a second penalty.
